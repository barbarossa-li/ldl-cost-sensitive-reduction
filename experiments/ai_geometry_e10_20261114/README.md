## Main interpretation

With NN5 otherwise fixed and allfour cells capacity matched, both construction and query weighting reduce equal-family mean Brier in all prespecified geometric/alpha.5/25-50-75% Full/cost40 conditional contrasts. Query effects are more consistent across families; construction improvements are not universal (Full25%, fixed uniform query:6better/1tie/9worse despite negative mean). Interaction is negative in the three Full budgets but positive atcost40 50/75%, so do not claim uniform synergy.
